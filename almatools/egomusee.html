FRAGMENTS
OF MEMORY

An exploration between matter, memory and imagination.

A visual experiment where physical objects become traces of imagined worlds.

The project

This project investigates the relationship between technology and the traces we leave behind. Images, objects and generated forms are combined to create a visual language somewhere between archaeology and fiction.

Each element is treated as a fragment rather than a finished object. Together they suggest a larger story without ever completely revealing it.

  • Mixed digital and physical processes
  • Original images and generated forms
  • Experimental material research
  • Combination of photography, sculpture and computation
  • Designed as an evolving installation
